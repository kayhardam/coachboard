# The board's components

Read with the root `AGENTS.md`, when a file in this folder is read; `CLAUDE.md` here is a symlink to this file.

`BoardEditor.svelte` (the editor, on `/<lang>/board/`) and `BoardViewer.svelte` (the viewer, on `/<lang>/board/link/` and `/<lang>/board/qr/`). How the board's JS, CSS and pages are built is under The board in the root `AGENTS.md`; My boards' storage is in `src/lib/board/AGENTS.md`.

## `BoardEditor.svelte`

- the QR dialog notes a code past version 30 (a link over 1732 characters, the largest the scan test read), and a board past version 40 gets a notice instead of a code;
- its CSS is a stylesheet, `BoardEditor.css`, imported by `BoardPage.astro` only, so Astro links it on the editor's page only and it stays out of the board's JS. Svelte doesn't scope it: every rule starts at `.editor`, or at `.qr` or `.boards` for the dialogs next to it (the QR code, My boards). Keep its styles out of the `.svelte` file: a `<style>` there would go into the bundle of every page, or into the JS with `css="injected"`;
- a title bar takes the site header's place: Home, the title (tap to edit, `setTitle()` in `edit.ts`), Undo, Share and More. More (a `<details>`) holds My boards and New board first, then Save to My boards (a received board only), the QR code, the court size and the three ways to clear, flat, so the T1 route with your own board stays two taps to a new board (in landscape in two columns, fixed to the bottom of the screen: nine items don't fit in 320 px of height). Delete shows only with a selection, over the top right of the court's box (in landscape: below the right panel; wide: below the tools), so it never moves or covers the court. Delete step sits in More, with more than one step;
- six tools, keys 1–6 (not while typing in a field): Move, Arrow, Attack, Defend, Ball, Cone. Arrow is the tool on opening: a tap picks a piece, a drag draws, from a player their run, from the ball a pass of the player nearest it (`holder()` in `hit.ts`, within twice a tap's reach, so always further than a tap reaches; nobody that near: a pass without a player). A new player, ball or cone isn't selected;
- smart arrows (decision 6, Kay, 9 October 2026): who receives a pass or bounce has the ball (`hasBall()` in `edit.ts`: it ends where they stand or where their run ends by then, in drawing order), and a drag from them is their pass; who has the ball at the start still runs, and who passed runs again (pass and go). A drag released near a player, at their spot or their run's end, ends there. A pass of a player released in a goal (`inGoal()` in `hit.ts`: between the posts, within a tap's reach of the goal line) is a shot; a pass without a player stays a pass. With Arrow a press on a player goes before a handle on them, so a chain of passes costs no tap; Move keeps the handle;
- one bar over the court (one block, `strip` in the editor): the kinds for a selected arrow (`setKind()`), the teams for a selected player (`setTeam()`), and on a new board (first visit, New board, the open board deleted) the starting lineups (`setup()` in `defaults.ts`) until its first edit. Picking a lineup saves nothing. In portrait and landscape it sits over the bottom of the court's box, wide under the list of steps (which then scrolls, so the bar fits). The lineups have no visible heading: it would become the LCP, after the JS;
- the steps (phase 14-2, decisions D1–D3 in the product plan): a step bar (`.steps`: a button per step, New step, key N; ← → go between steps, not while typing) and the sentence of the step on the court (`.text`; tap to edit, Enter keeps, Escape drops; New step, another step, Share, Undo or the court keep a sentence being typed first, `keepText()`). Undo returns to the step of the change. Where a button's label doesn't fit (320 px, or six steps), New step shows only its plus: its `aria-label` and `title` keep its name;
- Play (phase 14-3, decision D4), after New step, only its icon (`aria-label` and `title`: Play, Stop): from step 1, each step shows still (1.2 s), then its players move along their arrows to where the next step starts (1 s); the last step's move ends where its arrows take them. `moment()` in `src/lib/board/play.ts` (pure, unit-tested) gives the moment: exactly the step at 0 and the next step at 1; a player or ball the next step has elsewhere (moved by hand) goes straight; a ball goes with whoever has it and along each pass (`ballTrips()` in `moves.ts`, which `ballsAfter()` uses too). The arrows happen in turns (`turns()`): a player's arrows one after another, in drawing order; a pass waits for its ball, and whoever receives one waits for it before their next arrow; who needn't wait starts at once; the turns share the second of the move. Only to watch: the moment is `shown`, kept out of `board` like `draft`, so nothing reaches the link, My boards or Undo, and its places needn't be whole decimetres. A tap on the court (which then does nothing else), a tap on the sentence, another step, New step, Undo, the three ways to clear and the court size (`clearWith()`, which also keeps the step within the board: the default lineup and an empty court have one step), opening a board or Stop stops it first. With `prefers-reduced-motion` the board goes from step to step without moving. Wide, Play sits beside New step under the list;
- the court fits the space the bars leave (letterboxed), in three layouts: portrait (title bar, step bar, sentence, court, tools: the step bar above the sentence, so New step stays above a phone's keyboard); a phone in landscape (tools panel left, title panel right with the sentence at its bottom, both with labels; the step bar under the court; the sentence on four lines, two on a screen under 335 px high, a container query on `.editor`); wide (title bar across, tools column left, the steps right as a list, each step with its sentence, in a column of `--board-wide-steps`). `e2e/layout.spec.ts` checks that every button stays on screen and is at least 44 px high;
- the static fallback in `BoardPage.astro` reserves the bars' space with the same tokens (`--board-title`, `--board-steps`, `--board-text`, `--board-bar`, `--board-gap`, `--board-tools`, `--board-panel`, `--board-wide-side`, `--board-wide-steps` in `tokens.css`), so the court doesn't move when the editor replaces it, and has the title bar's Home link, for the way back without JavaScript. It also shows the placeholders of the title and the sentence, the largest texts, so the LCP paints with the HTML; Change the editor's box and the fallback's together; `e2e/layout.spec.ts` allows 1 px;
- its strings come in as a prop from `boardStrings()` in `ui.ts`, the paths for Share and the QR code as `links`, and the default lineup in the page's language as `lineup` (`defaultBoardFor(lang)` in `src/lib/board/defaults.ts`: LW, LB, CB, RB, RW, P, GK in English; LH, LO, MO, RO, RH, CL, K in Dutch). Astro passes props as `$state`, a proxy that `structuredClone()` can't copy, so the editor copies `lineup` once with JSON;
- **a folder's ⋯** (beside its name, not in the `<h3>`: a `<details>` can't sit in a heading) has Share folder and Duplicate folder. Share folder shares one message (`send()`, which Share uses too: the share sheet, else the clipboard): the folder's name, then each board's title and its link to the viewer (`/board/link/`), in the folder's order. Duplicate folder (`copyFolder()`) copies every board, in order and with its title, dated now, so the copy shows on top, named "<name> (copy)", then "(copy 2)" (`board.copySuffixN`), cut short before the suffix (`uniqueName()`), with its name field open. Both first save the board on the court if its save is still waiting;
- a board enters My boards at its first **real** change: when its link (`encode()`) differs from the board as it was opened. So the first visit, New board, a received link and "Open in the board" save nothing until you draw; "Edit a copy" from the viewer saves at once (see `BoardViewer.svelte`). A received board can be saved as it is with Save to My boards in More, which then goes away; without storage it says nothing. A board in the list gets a new date only when it changes;
- every change is written to the address bar (300 ms debounce), so it is always a shareable link. Next to it, not in it, the tab's history keeps the id of the board (`history.state.board`) and the step on the court (`history.state.step`: a reload opens that step; a link, a QR code or a board from My boards opens step 1): a reload or Back opens that board from My boards, so a reload never makes a copy, while a link, a QR code or a library item opens without an id and stays a received board. Without the id (a browser that lost the tab's history), a link that draws the board last opened is that board;
- it loads that board first, then `#t=` (a received board), then the board last opened, then `#own=` ("Moved from workers.dev" under The board in the root `AGENTS.md`), then the default lineup;
- opening another board (from the list, New board, a pasted link) first saves the board it leaves, if its save is still waiting, and starts a new undo history;
- a link from a newer version (`isNewerLink()`) reloads the page, on opening and in an open tab, because a tab opened before a deploy runs the old code. The same link reloads a tab only once (`coachboard.reloaded` in `sessionStorage`); after that, or without storage, it gets the notice for a broken link. A broken link of a known version (`1.…` or `2.…`) doesn't reload.

## `BoardViewer.svelte`

**`BoardViewer.svelte`** (phase 15, decisions K1–K13 in the product plan; it runs `client:only` on the viewer's pages).

- it shows the board in `#t=` step by step and changes nothing: no link, no My boards, no Undo. Its strings come from `viewerStrings()` in `ui.ts` (the `viewer.*` keys and the editor's it shares), its icons from `src/lib/viewerIcons.ts` (a module of their own, which `boardIcons` spreads, so the viewer doesn't carry the editor's);
- a title bar with Home, the title (none for a board without one) and "Edit a copy"; the step's sentence with "2/4"; the court; a legend (`legendOf()` in `src/lib/board/viewer.ts`: a passer and a cone if the board has one, then the kinds of arrow on it, in a fixed order); Previous, Play and Next under the thumb;
- Next moves the players along their arrows to the next step (`moment()` in `play.ts`); on the last step it plays that step's own move (the shot) and the board stays there, with Next off; Previous goes back a step, or, after the last move or during a move, to the start of the step. Play goes from step 1 to the end, also with one step. Where nothing moves (`moves()` in `viewer.ts`: every player and ball ends the move where it starts) nothing waits (Kay, 15 October 2026): Next goes on at once, Play gives the step only its time to read, Next is off on a last step that doesn't move, and a board of one step that doesn't move (a lineup) has Play and Next off. Each step shows still long enough to read its sentence and moves a share per turn of its arrows (`stillFor()`, `moveFor()` in `viewer.ts`: 60 ms a character, at least 1.2 s; 0.7 s a turn, at least 1 s); the editor keeps its own timing. A tap on the court, Stop or another control stops a move. With `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves. ← → go to the next and previous step;
- the sentence of the step is `aria-live`, so a screen reader hears each step. Wide, where the list of steps shows every sentence, it is hidden only to the eye, never with `display: none`;
- "Edit a copy" links to the editor with the same `#t=` and leaves that link in `sessionStorage` (`coachboard.copy`). The editor removes the key at once, whatever it holds, and only when it equals the `#t=` in the address bar keeps the board in My boards at once (or opens the same board, `sameBoard()`, if it is already there), with its title as it was. Without storage it opens as a received board;
- a link from a newer version reloads once, as in the editor (`reloadForNewer()` in `src/lib/board/newer.ts`, which both use); a broken link shows the notice on an empty court. Without `#t=` the page sends you to the editor (`location.replace`). A link pasted into an open tab (`hashchange`) opens there;
- its CSS is a stylesheet, `BoardViewer.css`, linked by `ViewerPage.astro` only; every rule starts at `.viewer`. The fallback uses the same classes, so its box is the viewer's: an empty half court, no default lineup (test 16), the controls disabled, and Home. The legend's row always holds two lines (`--viewer-legend`), the most a full legend takes on a 320 px phone, because the fallback can't know how many the board needs. Three layouts: portrait (title bar, sentence, court, legend, controls), a phone in landscape (L1: left Home, Edit a copy, the legend and Previous; right the title, the sentence, Play and Next) and wide (W2: every step with its sentence beside the court, the one on the court marked). "Edit a copy" shows its label from 360 px; below, only the pencil (`aria-label` and `title`). `e2e/layout.spec.ts` checks every layout with eight steps of `MAX_TEXT` and a title of `MAX_TITLE`;
- the LCP of these pages is the step's sentence, painted after the JS: the board is in the fragment, which the HTML can't know. `modulepreload` brings that JS in early.
